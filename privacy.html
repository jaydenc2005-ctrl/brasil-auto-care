Privacy

This explains what information Brasil Auto Care has about you and what happens to it. "I" and "me" mean Bruno Brasil, the owner, acting for Brasil Auto Care LLC.

What this website collects

Nothing that identifies you. The site has no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Booking happens by text message from your own phone.

What you give me when you text, call or book

  • Your phone number and name
  • Your vehicle's year, make and model, and photos of it
  • Your town, and the address where the work will be done
  • The content of our messages
  • What is needed to take your payment

How I use it

  • To quote, schedule and carry out your service
  • To send you before and after photos, a check-in after the job, and a reminder when the car is due again
  • To keep the business and tax records the law requires

Photos and video of your vehicle

I photograph and film vehicles before and after the work. Some of that footage appears on this site and on Brasil Auto Care's social media. I do not post your name or your address, and I avoid showing license plates. If you would rather your vehicle not appear, tell me at any time and I will not post it, or will take it down.

Who I share it with

I do not sell your information and I do not share it for advertising. It is stored in the ordinary tools I use to run the business, such as my phone, cloud storage and bookkeeping software, and I will disclose it if the law requires me to.

How long I keep it

For as long as you are a customer, and afterward for as long as tax and business records must be kept.

Your choices

Text or email me to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you. I will delete what I am not required by law to keep. Reply STOP to any text and I will stop messaging you.

Children

This site and service are not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use tracking pixels or analytics.

If that changes, for example if a live Google reviews display is added, this section will be updated before the change, and you will be asked for consent wherever the law requires it.

Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to use this site. It is built toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1, Level AA. If something does not work for you, call or text (203) 556-1023 or email brasilautocarect@gmail.com and I will help you directly and fix the problem.
