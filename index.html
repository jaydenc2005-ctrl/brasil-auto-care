Hey, I'm Bruno Brasil, the owner of Brasil Auto Care and the person who'll personally be taking care of your car.

This started the way a lot of good things do: with something that bugged me. I've always loved keeping my cars clean, going back to my first car, a 2014 Chevy Cruze. There was just something about driving a spotless car, getting compliments on how it looked and smelled, taking a regular car and making it feel like something special. But every time I ran my car through a drive-thru wash, I'd look at it in the sunlight afterward and just feel let down. Swirl marks. Water spots. Little scratches from those big brushes grinding dirt into the paint. It felt wrong to watch something I cared about get worn down by places that were just rushing through it.

So I decided to learn how to do it right. I spent hours studying, watching detailers, learning safe wash techniques, and understanding the products, the chemicals, the tools, and how to approach each vehicle the right way. A close friend who details his own cars helped me get started, and from there it turned into something I couldn't stop chasing: getting a little better with every car, paying attention to the details most places skip.

I'm not trying to be the fastest or the cheapest.

That's really the whole idea behind Brasil Auto Care. I'm the guy you call when you actually care about your car and want it treated with real attention: proper foam pre-soak, safe wash technique, no swirls, no shortcuts. The wheels cleaned front and back. The spots other people rush past. Done properly, every time.

And because I'm fully mobile, I bring all of that right to your driveway. You don't lift a finger. I come to you, and you get back a car that genuinely looks and feels new.

I treat every car like it matters, because to me, it does. If that's the kind of care you want for your car, I'd love to take care of it for you.

— Bruno Brasil
