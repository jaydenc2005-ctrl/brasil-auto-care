Add-ons, and when they're worth it

Seat or carpet shampoo and extraction

Vacuuming lifts loose dirt. This scrubs the fabric and pulls the dirty water back out. Add it for stains, spills, road salt, or seats that look darker than they should.

$50

Odor removal

An ozone treatment for smoke, pet and mildew smells that works on the source instead of covering it with fragrance. How well it holds depends on what is causing the smell. Add it when the smell comes back a day after cleaning.

$50

Trim restoration

Brings faded grey exterior plastic back to black. On an older car it changes the look more than anything else for the money.

Common questions

How do I book?

Tap any Book now button. It opens a text to me with the details I need already laid out. Fill in your car, attach three photos (front, one side, and the worst corner of the interior) and send. I reply with your price and we pick a day.

Do you need my water or an outlet?

No. The van carries its own water tank and generator. I only need room to park beside your car.

How long does each service take?

About 4 hours for a Full Detail, 2.5 hours for an Interior Detail and 1.5 hours for an Exterior Detail. A car in heavy condition takes longer.

Will the price change when you see the car?

Only if the car is in worse shape than the photos showed, and only by the amounts listed in the services section. I tell you before I start and you decide.

Where do you work?

Fairfield County and surrounding areas. Text me your town and I will tell you.

How does the maintenance plan work?

It starts after a Full Detail. I come back monthly or every two weeks for an exterior hand wash, wheels and glass, and an interior vacuum and wipe-down. There is no contract and you can stop with a text.

Are you booking for spring?

Yes. Text SPRING to (203) 556-1023 to join the waitlist. Waitlist customers get a free add-on with their first spring detail, and a $25 deposit locks a date and comes off the final bill.
